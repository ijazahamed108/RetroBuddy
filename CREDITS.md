## Emulation

- **[EmulatorJS](https://github.com/EmulatorJS/EmulatorJS)** v4.2.3 (GPL-3.0): the in-browser emulator frontend, loaded at runtime from `cdn.emulatorjs.org`. It is not modified or redistributed in this repository.
- **[libretro](https://www.libretro.com) cores**, compiled to WebAssembly by EmulatorJS. Each core has its own license, shown in the emulator's settings menu. They include:
  - [FCEUmm](https://github.com/libretro/libretro-fceumm) (NES), GPL-2.0
  - [Gambatte](https://github.com/libretro/gambatte-libretro) (Game Boy / Color), GPL-2.0
  - [mGBA](https://github.com/libretro/mgba) (Game Boy Advance), MPL-2.0
  - [Snes9x](https://github.com/libretro/snes9x), [Genesis Plus GX](https://github.com/libretro/Genesis-Plus-GX), [Mupen64Plus-Next](https://github.com/libretro/mupen64plus-libretro-nx), [melonDS](https://github.com/libretro/melonDS), [PCSX ReARMed](https://github.com/libretro/pcsx_rearmed) and others for custom ROMs, under their respective licenses.
