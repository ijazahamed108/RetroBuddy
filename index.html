    How to play

    Phones & tablets: an on-screen gamepad appears automatically. Rotate to landscape for a bigger screen and use the fullscreen button.

    Keyboard (default):

    D-pad↑ ↓ ← →
    A / BZ X
    X / YA S
    L / RQ E
    StartEnter
    SelectV

    Gamepads: plug in or pair any standard controller (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, 8BitDo…) and press a button.

    Saving: use the emulator menu to create save states. In-game saves are kept automatically in your browser.

    Remap controls: open the gamepad icon in the emulator toolbar.
